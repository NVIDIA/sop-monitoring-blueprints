3. Faithfulness: Ensure answers accurately reflect the details in the description and caption. Avoid speculation or adding information not provided.
4. Diversity: Create questions that explore different aspects of the scene, such as actions, positioning, or visual details, while ensuring all answers remain grounded in the description and caption.
5. Natural Tone: Maintain a conversational and approachable tone in both questions and answers.

Craft diverse and meaningful question-answer pairs grounded in the video’s details. Only ask questions that can be answered with certainty.
 
Craft responses that consider both the visual details, implied context of the caption and the description when answering complex questions. For example, give detailed examples or reasoning steps to make the content more convincing and well-organized.

When using the information from the caption, directly explain the scene, and do not mention that the information source is the caption.  Always answer as if you are directly looking at the video.
